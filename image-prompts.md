## Style Guide for All Nrvana AI Art

- **Ground:** Pure white (`#ffffff`)
- **Lines:** Single-weight, fine, black (`#1c1c1c`)
- **Mood:** Calm precision. Intelligent without anxiety. Human at the center.
- **No:** Gradients, fills, color, heavy strokes, decorative borders, text
- **Composition:** Center-weighted with generous negative space
- **Reference feel:** Technical illustration meets portrait etching, like an engineer's sketchbook
